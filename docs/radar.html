The open-weights model trails the closed flagship on every benchmark, most on FrontierMath Tier 4

Share of questions solved, 0–100%, for three models released in 2026. Axes run clockwise from the most saturated benchmark (top) to the hardest (upper left), so the gap to the flagship widens along the way: DeepSeek scores 0.46 on Tier 4 against 0.98 for GPT-6 Astra.

  • Not every closed model beats the open one: Gemini 3.7 Flash, a lighter closed model, scores below DeepSeek on FrontierMath Tier 4 (0.37 vs 0.46), so “closed” alone does not explain the gap; model tier matters.
  • Limits of the radar: axis order changes the shape (here: ordered by median score across the 40 2026 models with all six scores); the top two axes are almost saturated, so they separate nothing; benchmarks have different chance levels (GPQA 25%, chess puzzles about 5%).
  • Model names include the reasoning-effort setting used by Epoch (max, high, xhigh). Scores are single-run results; differences of a few points may be within noise.
