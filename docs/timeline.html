Fewer frontier AI models are open-weights: 39% in 2019–21, 11% since 2022

Each dot is a frontier model (top 5 by training compute when released), positioned by release date and by the lab that built it. Blue = weights downloadable, orange = closed.

  • Frontier follows Epoch AI’s definition: top 5 by training compute at release. It says nothing about capability.
  • Closed groups Epoch’s labels API access, hosted access, limited access and unreleased; open means downloadable weights.
  • Limit: the frontier list has only 7 models in 2025 and 1 in 2026, because many recent models have no compute estimate. The diamonds add 2026 models that Epoch labels limited-access or unreleased, taken from its separate Notable file (OpenAI, Anthropic and Google only).
  • Lanes use the first organisation listed for each model; Google, DeepMind, Brain and Research are merged. Descriptive only: the data do not show why models became closed.
