Language and multimodal models train with roughly 30–400× the compute of vision and biology models (medians)

Distribution of estimated training compute for notable AI models released since 2018, by domain. Each dot is a model; the shape shows where models cluster, the box marks the median and quartiles.

  • Only groups with at least 30 models are drawn (a violin of a small sample is mostly smoothing). Left out: Image generation (18), Games (12), Earth science (8), Speech (6), Robotics (5), Audio (5), Video (2), Mathematics (1), Other (1), Medicine (1), Recommendation (1).
  • Limit: only models with a compute estimate appear (about half of Epoch’s notable models), so this is not every model. Multi-domain models are assigned to Multimodal if listed, otherwise to their first-listed domain. The shape depends on the smoothing bandwidth (plot default); the density is cut at the data range.
  • Every violin has the same area whatever its n, so read n from the axis label.
